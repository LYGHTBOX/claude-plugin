# LYGHTBOX

Work with your LYGHTBOX studio from Claude or ChatGPT. Look up clients, projects and bookings, keep them up to date,
and prepare emails, campaigns and booking pages for you to review in LYGHTBOX. Moving from another platform, such as
HoneyBook or Dubsado, brings your clients and projects over with their payment history.

## Connect

1. Add LYGHTBOX in Claude or ChatGPT.
2. Sign in to LYGHTBOX when asked and choose the account. Leave **Allow edits** on if the assistant should be able to
   make changes.
3. Ask for what you need, such as "Which sessions do I have next week?"

You can see and end each connection in LYGHTBOX under **Settings → Connected apps**, along with every change it made.

## What it can do

- Find and read the contacts, projects, bookings, booking pages, campaigns, schedule and client messages your
  LYGHTBOX role can see.
- With edits allowed: add and update contacts and projects, save email and campaign drafts, prepare booking pages and
  message templates, and import contacts and projects from another platform.
- It doesn't send emails, launch campaigns, publish booking pages or charge anyone: you review its drafts and send
  them in LYGHTBOX. A booked project's automated emails follow its changes, as they would after the same change in
  LYGHTBOX, and upcoming bookings it imports get their service's emails as usual.

## Data

The assistant reaches your LYGHTBOX account through agents.lyghtbox.com, only for the account you choose when
connecting, and only within what your role allows. It receives the records it asks for to answer you. LYGHTBOX sees
only what the assistant sends it, never your conversation, and keeps a record of each change the assistant makes so
you can review it. See the [privacy policy](https://lyghtbox.com/privacy-policy).
